- **Exclusion:** no heavy red meat (beef, lamb, pork) during Phase 1.

## Atlas verification notes (not part of the protocol; for the Architect)

Checked 30 Sep 2026 by the Protocol Atlas.

1. **Citation 8.1 is mislabelled.** The title "Efficacy of a meal replacement diet plan compared to a food-based diet plan after a period of weight loss and weight maintenance: a randomized controlled trial" is Davis L.M. et al., *Nutrition Journal* 9:11 (2010). It tested the **Medifast** 5 & 1 plan and was supported by Medifast, not Herbalife, and not by Flechtner-Mors. Result: 12.3% vs 6.7% weight loss at 16 weeks. Source: https://nutritionj.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1475-2891-9-11. Do not cite it as proof for F1 + F3. Replace with a study that used Herbalife products, confirmed from the paper itself.
2. **The 15-20% figure in 8.2 has no specific study attached.** The post-lunch dip and circadian trough are real, but the percentage and the "insulin-driven serotonin/melatonin spike" need a named paper before they appear in copy. Until then, describe the dip without a number.
3. **Keep these out of public copy** (they read as disease or treatment claims under Malaysian advertising rules and distributor codes): "binds toxins", "turns off fires in joints and arteries", "forces vasodilation", "gut healing", "visceral fat oxidation", "Heart Firewall".
4. **Label vs protocol doses:** where the protocol exceeds the label (Tea, Niteworks), public copy should not state doses; they belong in the client's private plan.
5. **Caffeine math (4.0).** The protocol tea dose is 1 tsp, about 170 mg caffeine (2 x 85 mg). With a 5-6 hour half-life, about 85 mg is still in the blood at 10:00 pm, so "clear by 10:00 pm" does not hold. Options: 1/2 tsp at 4:00 pm, or move the tea to 2:00-3:00 pm.
